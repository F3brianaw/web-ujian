CASE STUDY / 04Finalist · UM
SUSTAINABILITY · FOOD WASTE

ZeroWaste

A platform that helps reduce food waste by connecting food businesses with people and partners who can make use of surplus food.

The challenge

Restaurants and small food businesses can have unsold or leftover food that still has value. Without a practical distribution or recycling route, these resources may become waste.

The design direction

Make it easier for businesses to redirect surplus food for consumption or recycling through two connected initiatives.

Two main features

Food Rescue — helps businesses distribute or resell surplus food so it can still be consumed.

Food Recycle — connects food waste with parties who can process it, such as maggot farmers.

Target users

UMKMRestaurantsFood recipientsMaggot farmers

CASE STUDY / 05SMART SYSTEM · IoT
PLANT MONITORING · AUTOMATION

PlantYuk

A smart plant care application connected to a monitoring interface and automated watering system.

The challenge

Plant care requires attention to soil moisture and temperature. Watering without considering these conditions can lead to overwatering or underwatering, while users may find sensor data difficult to interpret.

The design direction

Present technical data in a clear dashboard and translate environmental readings into understandable watering recommendations.

Key capabilities

Plant monitoringSoil moistureTemperatureWatering recommendationAutomated watering

The system uses fuzzy logic to determine a suitable watering duration based on environmental conditions.
